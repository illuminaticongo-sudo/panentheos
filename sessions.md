# Sessions

## Work with Jahn

Panentheos offers direct work through private sessions, consultation calls, and group breathwork experiences.

These sessions are for people seeking clarity, release, grounding, transformation, and a more direct relationship with breath, awareness, and spiritual life.

Available in Chicago and remotely.

## 1:1 Breathwork Sessions

Private sessions are the most direct way to enter the work.

A session may support:
- emotional release
- nervous system regulation
- spiritual opening
- clarity during transition
- reconnection with the body
- deeper contemplative grounding

Each session is guided with care and shaped around the person in front of me.

## Consultation Calls

If you are new to the work, a consultation is a simple place to begin.

This is a chance to ask questions, explore fit, and get clear on what kind of support or practice might be right for you.

## Group Sessions

Panentheos also offers group breathwork and shared practice spaces.

These may take the form of:
- guided group breathwork
- contemplative classes
- community practice gatherings
- special workshops or events

## What to expect

Sessions may include:
- guided breathing patterns
- grounding and awareness practices
- emotional release
- energetic movement
- silence and integration

Some sessions are gentle. Some are intense. The work is approached with respect, care, and spiritual seriousness.
