# About the Practice

## Panentheos

**God in All and All in God.**

Panentheos is a contemplative practice community, a home monastic support system for people seeking a deeper spiritual life while living fully in the world.

It brings together psychedelic breathwork, daily streaming, spiritual teaching, contemplative rhythm, and subscription-based study into one living path of practice.

## What is Panentheos?

Panentheos begins with a simple recognition:

**God is in all, and all is in God.**

This is not only an idea to believe.  
It is a reality to practice, remember, and live.

Panentheos exists to support that remembrance through breath, attention, study, discipline, and shared spiritual rhythm.

It is a place for people who want a real contemplative life, but who are not leaving the world behind to find one.

## A home monastic support system

Panentheos is meant to function like a home monastic support group.

Not a monastery in the traditional sense, but a structure of support for people trying to live with greater devotion, discipline, awareness, and depth in the middle of ordinary life.

It is for people who want:
- rhythm
- structure
- practice
- support
- spiritual companionship
- a place to return to

The goal is not escape from life, but transformation within it.

## More than breathwork

Breathwork is one important stream within Panentheos, but it is not the whole of it.

Panentheos includes:
- psychedelic breathwork
- daily live streams
- spiritual teaching
- contemplative practice
- subscription school
- private sessions and group work

It is not only a service.  
It is a way of life.

## Psychedelic breathwork

Panentheos often uses the phrase **psychedelic breathwork** to describe the revelatory power of breath itself.

Not psychedelic because of substances, but because breath can open perception, release emotional material, regulate the nervous system, deepen awareness, and reconnect people to what feels most sacred and real.

This work can support:
- clarity
- release
- regulation
- embodied presence
- spiritual depth
- deeper intimacy with God

Breath is one doorway among several, but it is a profound one.

## Rooted in lineage

Panentheos draws from three core streams:

**Rebirthing Breathwork**  
Study with Leonard Orr beginning in 2005.

**Kundalini Yoga**  
Teacher training through Spirit Rising Yoga, 2005 to 2006.

**Kemetic Yoga**  
Training with Yirser Ra Hotep beginning in 1995, with formal teacher training completed in 2014.

These are living roots that shape the practice and its integrity.

## What Panentheos offers

**Daily Streaming**  
A recurring rhythm of live practice, reflection, teaching, and spiritual companionship.

**Subscription School**  
Teachings, archives, guided study, and a deeper container for committed practice.

**Psychedelic Breathwork**  
Private and group sessions for awakening, release, regulation, and inner transformation.

**Teachings and Transmissions**  
Spoken, written, and embodied offerings shaped by decades of study and lived practice.

## Who this is for

Panentheos is for people who are seeking:
- a deeper spiritual life
- a stable rhythm of practice
- contemplative support in ordinary life
- breath-based transformation
- real study, not just inspiration
- guidance without performance or pretense

You do not need to have it all figured out.  
You do not need the perfect language.  
You do not need prior experience.

You only need willingness, sincerity, and a desire to practice.
