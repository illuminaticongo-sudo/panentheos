## What is Panentheos?

Panentheos is a path of psychedelic breathwork.

Not psychedelic because of substances, but because breath itself can open perception, dissolve fixed patterns, deepen presence, and reconnect us to what is most alive, sacred, and real.

This practice supports:
- nervous system regulation
- emotional release
- spiritual depth
- embodied clarity
- deeper connection to breath, body, and God

## Rooted in lineage

Panentheos draws from three primary streams:

**Rebirthing Breathwork**  
Study with Leonard Orr beginning in 2005.

**Kundalini Yoga**  
Teacher training through Spirit Rising Yoga, 2005 to 2006.

**Kemetic Yoga**  
Training with Yirser Ra Hotep beginning in 1995, with formal teacher training completed in 2014.

These lineages come together as one grounded, living practice.

## What to expect

Sessions may include guided breathing, grounding, emotional release, energetic movement, deep stillness, and integration.

Some experiences are subtle. Some are intense. The work is always guided with care.

You do not need prior experience. Only willingness.

## About Jahn Hooks

Jahn Hooks is a breathwork guide, artist, and writer based in Chicago. Through Panentheos, he offers psychedelic breathwork as a grounded spiritual practice shaped by more than two decades of study, teaching, and direct experience.
