# Panentheos Brand Core

## Core meaning
**Panentheos = God in All and All in God.**

## Core identity
Panentheos is a contemplative practice community and home monastic support system for people seeking a deeper spiritual life while living fully in the world.

It brings together:
- psychedelic breathwork
- daily streaming
- spiritual teaching
- contemplative rhythm
- subscription-based study
- private sessions and group work

## What Panentheos is not
Panentheos is not only:
- a breathwork booking site
- a content brand
- a spiritual influencer page
- a school without lived practice

It is a living spiritual ecosystem.

## Brand architecture
- **Illuminati Congo** = artist ecosystem
- **Panentheos** = contemplative and spiritual practice ecosystem

## Core language
Use phrases like:
- a home for contemplative practice in ordinary life
- a home monastic support system
- a living path of practice
- psychedelic breathwork
- God in All and All in God
- practice, rhythm, support, remembrance

## Tone
- calm
- sacred
- grounded
- intimate
- serious but humane
- disciplined without feeling rigid
- mystical, but not inflated

## Primary promise
Panentheos gives people a structure of support for living a real contemplative life in the world.
