# About Jahn

## Jahn Hooks

Jahn Hooks is a breathwork guide, artist, writer, and spiritual teacher based in Chicago.

Through Panentheos, he offers a living path of contemplative practice rooted in breath, study, discipline, and direct experience.

## A life of practice

Panentheos is not built from theory alone. It grows out of decades of lived work.

Jahn’s path includes long-term study and practice in:
- Rebirthing Breathwork
- Kundalini Yoga
- Kemetic Yoga
- spiritual writing and contemplative inquiry
- music as transmission and devotional art

## Lineage and formation

**Rebirthing Breathwork**  
Study with Leonard Orr beginning in 2005.

**Kundalini Yoga**  
Teacher training through Spirit Rising Yoga, 2005 to 2006.

**Kemetic Yoga**  
Training with Yirser Ra Hotep beginning in 1995, with formal teacher training completed in 2014.

## Why Panentheos

Panentheos brings together the major streams of Jahn’s life into one living practice ecosystem.

The music, the breathwork, the teaching, the writing, the daily discipline, and the spiritual search all belong to one body of work.

Through Panentheos, Jahn offers support for people seeking a deeper relationship with God, breath, awareness, and contemplative life in the world.
