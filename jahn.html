About Jahn

Breathwork guide.
Artist. Teacher.
Still going.

Jahn Hooks is a breathwork guide, artist, writer, and spiritual teacher based in Chicago. Through Panentheos, he offers a living path of contemplative practice rooted in breath, study, discipline, and direct experience.

A life of practice

Panentheos is not built from theory alone. It grows out of decades of lived work in breath, study, writing, music, contemplative inquiry, and spiritual discipline.

The music, the breathwork, the teaching, the writing, and the daily practice all belong to one body of work.

Lineage and formation

  • Rebirthing Breathwork, study with Leonard Orr beginning in 2005
  • Kundalini Yoga, teacher training through Spirit Rising Yoga, 2005 to 2006
  • Kemetic Yoga, training with Yirser Ra Hotep beginning in 1995, with formal teacher training completed in 2014
