About the Practice

God in All
and All in God.

Panentheos is a contemplative practice community and home monastic support system for people seeking a deeper spiritual life while living fully in the world. It supports remembrance through breath, attention, study, discipline, and shared spiritual rhythm.

It is for people who want a real contemplative life, but are not leaving the world behind to find one.

  • Home monastic support system
    Not monastery cosplay, but real support for devotion, discipline, awareness, and return inside ordinary life.
  • Psychedelic breathwork
    Breath can open perception, release emotional material, deepen awareness, and restore contact with what is sacred and real. Panentheos uses psychedelic breathwork as a direct, embodied practice of spiritual transformation.
  • Regular live streams and teachings
    Not pressure for daily output, but a steady recurring rhythm of practice, teaching, video content, and return.
  • Living roots
    Grounded in Rebirthing Breathwork, Kundalini Yoga, and Kemetic Yoga as living lineages, with psychedelic breathwork as part of the Panentheos public language.

School / Membership

A living school
for ordinary life.

The Panentheos School is the membership and study arm of the wider practice ecosystem. It is for people who want more than occasional inspiration. It is for people who want rhythm, guidance, structure, and ongoing formation.

This is not only a content library. It is a place to study, practice, return, and deepen over time.

  • What the school offers
    Live stream access, guided breathwork, archived teachings, structured study paths, reflections on spiritual life and discipline, and live gatherings.
  • Why join
    Most people do not need more scattered spiritual information. They need a container.
  • Who it serves
    People who want a home monastic rhythm without leaving the world, and a deeper structure of support for living a contemplative life.

About Jahn

Breathwork guide.
Artist. Teacher.
Still going.

Jahn Hooks, known musically as Illuminati Congo, is a psychedelic breathwork guide, artist, writer, and spiritual teacher based in Chicago. Through Panentheos, he offers a living path of contemplative practice rooted in breath, study, discipline, and direct experience.

Panentheos grows out of decades of lived work, not theory alone.

  • Rebirthing Breathwork
    Trained with Leonard Orr, founder of Rebirthing Breathwork, in the Sierra Nevada mountains in 2005. Ongoing study since. Primary breathwork lineage of the Panentheos practice.
  • Kundalini Yoga
    Teacher Training with Spirit Rising Yoga, 2005–2006.
  • Kemetic Yoga
    Training with Yirser Ra Hotep beginning in 1995. Formal teacher training completed in 2014.
  • Contemplative Study
    Years of personal study in Christian mysticism, Gnostic traditions, Rastafarian theology, and Kemetic philosophy — drawn from practice, reading, and direct transmission.
  • Music & Performance
    20+ years as Illuminati Congo — improvisation, presence, and sound as contemplative discipline.
