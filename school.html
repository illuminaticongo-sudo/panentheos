School / Membership

A living school for
ordinary life.

The Panentheos School is the membership and study arm of the wider practice ecosystem. It is for people who want rhythm, guidance, structure, and ongoing formation.

What the school offers

  • access to live streams
  • guided breathwork and contemplative practices
  • archived teachings and transmissions
  • structured study paths
  • reflections on spiritual life and discipline
  • live classes or member gatherings

Why join

Most people do not need more scattered spiritual information. They need a container.

The school exists to give people a deeper structure of support for living a real contemplative life in the world.

It is for people who want spiritual formation, not just inspiration.
