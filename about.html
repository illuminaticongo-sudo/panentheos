About the Practice

God in All
and All in God.

Panentheos is a contemplative practice community and home monastic support system for people seeking a deeper spiritual life while living fully in the world.

What is Panentheos?

Panentheos begins with a simple recognition: God is in all, and all is in God. This is not only an idea to believe. It is a reality to practice, remember, and live.

Panentheos exists to support that remembrance through breath, attention, study, discipline, and shared spiritual rhythm. It is a place for people who want a real contemplative life, but who are not leaving the world behind to find one.

A home monastic support system

Panentheos is meant to function like a home monastic support group, a structure of support for people trying to live with greater devotion, discipline, awareness, and depth in the middle of ordinary life.

The goal is not escape from life, but transformation within it.

Living roots
